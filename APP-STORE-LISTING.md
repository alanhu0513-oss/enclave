# App Store Listing — Enclave

## iOS App Store

### App Name
Enclave — Identity Protection

### Subtitle
Deepfake Detection & Monitoring

### Description
Protect your digital identity from AI-powered threats. Enclave monitors the web for unauthorized use of your face, voice, and personal information.

**KEY FEATURES**

🔍 **Deepfake Detection**
Scan images and videos for AI manipulation using our multi-layered ML pipeline. Cloud AI, Gemini, and XceptionNet work together for 94%+ accuracy.

🛡️ **Identity Monitoring**
Proactive web crawler searches search engines, social media, and dark web for your faceprints and voiceprints. Get alerted before threats spread.

⚡ **Real-Time Alerts**
Instant notifications via WebSocket, email, and push when threats are detected. Know immediately when your identity is compromised.

🚨 **Takedown Pipeline**
Auto-generate DMCA and Cease & Desist letters. Email platform abuse teams. Track takedown lifecycle from submission to resolution.

🌐 **Browser Extensions**
Deepfake Radar analyzes images on any website. Video Call Shield detects deepfakes during live calls.

📊 **Analytics Dashboard**
Track threats, scan history, and detection accuracy. Monitor your digital footprint in real-time.

🔒 **Enterprise Ready**
SSO, organizations, audit logs, and white-label branding for teams and businesses.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Family Plans**
Protect your entire family with shared monitoring and consolidated alerts.

**WHY ENCLAVE?**

• $0 budget — free tier with 100 scans/month
• Privacy-first — your data never leaves your device without permission
• Multi-layered AI — 5 detection providers for maximum accuracy
• Real-time — WebSocket-powered instant alerts
• Open API — integrate with your existing security stack

**PERFECTION**
