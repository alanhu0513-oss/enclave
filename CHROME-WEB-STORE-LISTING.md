## Extension 1: ENCLAVE Deepfake Radar

**Name:** ENCLAVE Deepfake Radar
**Short Description:** Detect deepfakes in social media feeds in real-time
**Category:** Social & Communication
**Language:** English

### Description (4096 char max)
ENCLAVE Deepfake Radar scans images in your social media feeds (Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, Facebook, YouTube) for signs of AI manipulation and deepfakes.

**How it works:**
• Automatically scans images as you scroll through social feeds
• Uses AI-powered analysis to detect face swaps, voice clones, and synthetic media
• Shows risk badges on suspicious images with confidence scores
• Optional: Connect your Enclave account for cloud-powered ML detection

**Features:**
• Real-time feed scanning with MutationObserver
• Sensitivity controls (Low / Medium / High)
• Badge overlay toggle for subtle or visible warnings
• API detection mode for higher accuracy (requires free Enclave account)
• Local heuristic fallback — works offline
• Stats tracking (images scanned, threats found)
• Zero data collection — all analysis happens on your device

**Privacy:**
• No browsing history collected
• No images sent to third parties (unless API mode enabled with your consent)
• All data stored locally in your browser

## Extension 2: ENCLAVE Video Call Shield

**Name:** ENCLAVE Video Call Shield
**Short Description:** Real-time deepfake detection during video calls
**Category:** Social & Communication
**Language:** English

### Description (4096 char max)
ENCLAVE Video Call Shield protects you from deepfakes during live video calls on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex.

**How it works:**
• Captures video frames every 2 seconds during calls
• Analyzes frames for signs of AI manipulation or face swapping
• Shows real-time alerts if a deepfake is detected
• Shield indicator shows protection status

**Features:**
• Works on Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, and Webex
• Real-time frame capture and analysis
• Configurable sensitivity (Low / Medium / High)
• Auto-scan mode — no manual intervention needed
• Chrome notifications for threat alerts
• Scan history and statistics
• Connect to Enclave API for cloud-powered ML detection

**Privacy:**
• Video frames are processed locally (unless API mode enabled)
• No call recordings stored
• No browsing history collected
• All analysis happens on your device
